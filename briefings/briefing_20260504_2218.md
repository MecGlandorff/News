For anti-abortion litigants, the mail system and telehealth rules are a way to challenge federal regulatory authority and cross-state distribution.

The key point is that the Supreme Court has not resolved the underlying merits. The next week will matter procedurally: lower-court deadlines, emergency applications and any further Supreme Court order will determine whether mail delivery remains available or is again suspended while the broader legal challenge proceeds.
