**Status:** Escalating | **Confidence:** High | **Source agreement:** Broad | **Dispute:** None

**What changed today:** An official Finance Ministry warning turned the 2031 expiry of mortgage-interest deductions into an administrability crisis the cabinet cannot cleanly postpone.

### Why it matters
The Dutch mortgage-interest fight has moved from ideology to tax-system dysfunction. Finance Ministry officials warn that from 2031 many homeowners lose the right to the 30-year maximum mortgage-interest deduction, but no central body knows exactly who they are. NOS says the problem mainly concerns roughly one million pre-2013, wholly or partly interest-only mortgages. Banks, advisers and the Tax Administration may not have kept the necessary long-term records; taxpayers themselves often cannot reconstruct decades of moves, divorces and loan parts. Politically, the issue lands inside a divided coalition: VVD wants to preserve the deduction, while D66 and CDA are more willing to reduce it. NRC reports the cabinet still appears inclined to push a decision onward, despite new warnings.
