# ADR 0020: Current evidence versus historical context

**Date:** 2026-07-11

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Loading every claim ever attached to a story allowed old, high-confidence claims to displace today's evidence and make stale values look like present corroboration or conflict.

## Decision

Evidence briefings may receive claims from the current editorial day plus the previous six days. Older claims are explicitly marked `historical_context` and dated. Only current-day claims may determine present-tense source agreement or source-divergence labels.

Exact and conservative similar-claim repetition describe multi-source support, not independent corroboration. Precise numeric, date, status, and attribution differences may produce `mixed` / `possible conflict`; they never produce confirmed-contradiction prose.

## Consequences

- Historical memory remains useful for continuity and delta explanations.
- Old claims cannot strengthen today's agreement or create a current conflict by themselves.
- Source identity still does not establish editorial independence or truth.
