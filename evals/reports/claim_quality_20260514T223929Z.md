## Case Notes

### `riverbend_dam_evacuation`

RSS matched the evacuation-order claim only. Full text matched all three expected claims:

- evacuation order near North Dam
- spillway closure until Monday
- reservoir rise forecast of 30 centimeters

The full-text output also produced two evacuation-related claims. They are not identical duplicates, but they are close enough that downstream source agreement should avoid treating them as independent corroboration.

### `geneva_ceasefire_talks`

RSS matched the Geneva meeting claim only. Full text matched the Geneva meeting claim and the no-shelling-after-06:00 claim.

The model split the artillery allegation and denial into separate claims. That is more atomic than the dataset expectation `artillery_allegation_denied`, which combines allegation and denial in one expected item. The eval therefore records that expected item as unmatched even though the output captured both parts separately.

The important failure was the denial claim:

```text
claim_text: Lydora denied the Marek foreign ministry's allegation about moving artillery near the Kars crossing.
evidence_span: a claim Lydora denied.
```

The span appears in the article, but by itself it is anaphoric. It does not contain the artillery, Marek foreign ministry, or Kars crossing details. The previous deterministic derivability gate accepted it because `Lydora` appeared in the span.

## Conclusion

Full text improves coverage in this fixture report, and basic evidence-span containment stayed clean. However, this report exposed that entity-overlap deterministic acceptance was too permissive for short anaphoric spans.

Follow-up implemented in the claim-derivability branch:

- Verbatim claim/span containment still accepts deterministically.
- Number mismatches still reject deterministically.
- Entity-overlap acceptance now requires stronger non-entity lexical overlap.
- Weak entity-only overlap routes to the cheap verifier instead of being accepted deterministically.

## Remaining Gap

This eval still does not directly measure verifier accuracy on reviewed paraphrase cases or real article text. The next planned review is a 10-case real news eval that should include:

- real RSS and full-text inputs from recent evidence runs
- reviewed expected claims
- cases where deterministic checks return `uncertain`
- verifier accept/reject decisions as the deciding signal
- notes on whether full-text coverage gain is worth the added cost and latency

Until that review exists, do not treat this fixture report as enough evidence to broaden claim-backed source agreement or source-divergence behavior.
